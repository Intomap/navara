# Hong Kong city demo data

This directory contains runtime artifacts only. Offline source-data preparation and compilation tools are not part of this demo.

- Buildings, roads, street furniture and sign records: Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region open data and OpenStreetMap contributors, as identified in the accompanying `manifest.json` and `sources.json`.
- Orthophotography: Lands Department, HKSAR (TDOP). See `ortho/index.json`.

Retain the dataset-specific notices in `manifest.json` and `sources.json` when redistributing these artifacts. OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Database License; Hong Kong government datasets remain subject to their respective data.gov.hk terms.
